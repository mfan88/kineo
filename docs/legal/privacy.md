# Privacy policy

## Who we are

Kineō is an app for physiotherapists in British Columbia to write visit notes and send invoices. It is made by fenna.tech, a business name of M2 Investments Corporation, in British Columbia, Canada ("we", "us").

This policy explains what information Kineō handles, where it goes and what you can do about it. "You" means the physiotherapist who uses Kineō. We handle personal information in line with British Columbia's _Personal Information Protection Act_ (PIPA).

## What we collect

**About you, the practitioner:**

- **Your account:** your email address, and your name and picture if you choose "Continue with Google". This is held by Clerk, our sign-in provider (see "Who we share it with").
- **Your profile and settings:** the name, title, clinic, address and phone number you enter in Your profile, your price list, tax rate, invoice number settings and invoice style, including any logo you upload.
- **That you agreed to these documents:** when you create an account, the date you agreed to the Terms of use and this policy.
- **Technical information:** when the apps connect to our server, it sees your device's IP address and the time of each request. Our server's logs never record your sign-in tokens or the content of your notes.

**About your patients**, which you enter: see "Patient information".

**What we don't collect:** the apps have no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Our website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. We don't sell or rent anyone's information.

## Patient information

Kineō stores the patient information you enter: names, reasons for visit, visit dates and notes, billing addresses, funding details such as a funder's claim or billing number, and invoices.

**You are responsible for your patients' information.** As the physiotherapist, you collect it, decide what to record and decide who receives it. We act as your service provider: we store and sync it so you can use Kineō, and we don't use it for anything else.

- We never contact your patients, and we never send them invoices. You send invoices yourself, as PDFs.
- Invoices never include note text.
- We don't look at patient information, except to fix a problem you ask us to fix, to keep the service running and secure, or when the law requires it. If the law requires us to disclose it, we'll tell you first unless the law forbids that.
- We never use patient information to train software, for marketing, or to build profiles.

## Where your data is stored

- **On your devices.** Each device you sign in on keeps a copy of your practice, so Kineō works without an internet connection. Signing out removes it from that device. On a phone, it's protected by the phone's own encryption, which is on whenever the phone has a passcode. On a computer, it's protected by your computer's disk encryption (FileVault on a Mac, BitLocker on Windows); turn that on.
- **On our server, in British Columbia, Canada.** Changes you make are sent over an encrypted connection to a server we operate in British Columbia. If we move to another host, your data will stay in Canada, and we'll update this policy first.
- **Backups.** We back up the server's data every night. Backups are encrypted, and only we hold the key that can read them. They're kept in Canada.
- **Your sign-in details** are held by Clerk, in the United States (see below). Patient information never goes to Clerk.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with these companies, only for what's listed, and never patient information:

- **Clerk** (clerk.com), our sign-in provider, in the United States. It holds your email address, sign-in history and, if you use Google, your name and picture from Google. It also runs checks to block automated sign-up abuse. Because Clerk is in the United States, US law applies to the information it holds.
- **Google**, only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google tells Clerk your name, email address and picture, and learns that you signed in to Kineō.
- **Apple and Google's app stores**, which deliver the phone app under their own privacy policies.

Apart from these, we disclose information only if the law requires it, as described in "Patient information".

## How long we keep it

- **While your account is open,** we keep your practice so you can use it on any of your devices.
- **When you close your account,** we delete your practice from our server **90 days** later. The 90 days let you change your mind or ask us for a copy. Our encrypted backups are replaced within **14 days** after that, so nothing remains.
- **Your sign-in details** at Clerk are deleted when we delete your practice.
- **Your own records:** your College requires you to keep clinical and billing records for a set time. Kineō isn't a records archive. Before you close your account, ask us for a copy of your practice, or keep your invoices as PDFs.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See and correct** your information at any time in the apps: your patients, notes, invoices and profile.
- **Get a copy** of your practice. Email us and we'll send it to you within 30 days.
- **Close your account** by emailing us. We'll confirm, and delete your practice as described in "How long we keep it".
- **Ask what information we hold about you**, and how we've used it. We'll answer within 30 days.
- **Complain.** Contact us first. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

Patients who ask about their information should contact their physiotherapist. If a patient contacts us, we'll refer them to you, and help you answer.

## Security

- Every connection between the apps and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Our server lets each practitioner reach only their own practice. This is enforced by the database itself, not just the apps.
- Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage (the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager on a computer, the Keychain or Keystore on a phone).
- Backups are encrypted, and only we can read them.
- The server sits behind a firewall, is kept up to date, and is reached for administration only from our own devices.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information or your patients', we'll tell you promptly. We'll give you what you need to meet your own obligations, including notifying patients if that's required.

Please help: use a passcode on your phone, turn on your computer's disk encryption, and sign out of Kineō on any device you stop using.

## Children

Kineō is for physiotherapists, not for children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 19 as a user. Your patients may include children. Their information is yours to handle, including consent from a parent or guardian where your College's standards require it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters, such as a new company handling your information or a change in where it's stored, we'll tell you in the app or by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.
