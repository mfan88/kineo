# Kineō design brief

Product name: **Kineō** (display name, single character ō). Use plain **kineo** for every identifier: repo, packages, bundle IDs, URLs, file names.

v2 storyboard (phone and desktop note and invoice flows): [Kineō v2 storyboard](https://claude.ai/artifact/NsrUrTUR5PDWYH3b5jFh45) (private until shared from its Share menu). Research behind it: [docs/research/notes-and-invoicing-workflows.md](research/notes-and-invoicing-workflows.md).

## Who

- Solo physiotherapist, British Columbia first
- Native app on desktop and phone (not a website)

## Navigation

- Desktop: **no top bar and no sidebar.** Everything flows from Home. A quiet tab bar sits in the window's top-right corner: **Home** and **Invoices** as text links (the current one in the text colour and semibold, the other muted) and the settings icon; patients are found from Home's search (owner decision, U10). Every screen but Home has a back arrow at the top left of its content and at most one action at the top right of its content. The Kineō wordmark (24px) sits in the window's top-left corner. Settings and its screens show neither corner (see Settings below).
- Phone: bottom tab bar (Home, Patients, Invoices, Settings), active tab sky blue. Detail screens (patient, new invoice, invoice, preview, invoice detail, new patient, profile, price list) show a back arrow. Extra top padding (64px; back arrow row 52px) for the status bar and notch. 16px side margins; the screen's primary button is pinned to the bottom, above the tab bar where there is one.

## Screens

The v2 storyboard shows each screen; this list is the written version.

1. **Home:** "Who are you seeing?", patient search, **Recent** as list rows (name, "Last visit 4 Nov"), then **To do** count cards: Ready to invoice (N visits) and Waiting on payment (N invoices). Draft invoices are found in the Invoices list, not on Home. Ready to invoice opens the visit picker (one patient waiting) or the Ready to invoice list; Waiting on payment always opens the Invoices list on its Waiting on payment group. Desktop uses the same sections in its current layout.
2. **Patients:** live search, "New patient" button, "No patients match" empty state offering to add the name.
3. **New patient:** Full name (required), Reason for visit (optional), then opens the patient's note.
4. **Patient:** name, "Last visit · reason"; note box first, titled with today's date ("Today, 12 Nov"), typing or dictation, with "Add to note" / "Replace note" when today's visit already has a note; primary **Save note**. Visit list with status tags, a one-line note excerpt and "Edited 10:42"; visits on a paid invoice are marked locked. Under the note, "Visit date: today" with **Change date** (a "Visit date" field, typed like "11 Nov 2026"; the title becomes "Visit on 11 Nov"). Each visit not on an invoice has **Change date** (dialog on desktop, sheet on phone, "Visit date changed") and **Delete visit** ("Visit deleted" with Undo, kept until dismissed). **Edit details** (desktop: beside Create invoice; phone: "Edit" beside the name) corrects the full name and reason for visit ("Patient updated"). After saving, the primary becomes **Create invoice** while the patient has visits not yet invoiced. Desktop: two columns (note left with a quiet "Saved on this device · edited 10:42" line, visits right) and "Create invoice (N visits)" at the top right.
5. **New invoice (phone):** "For [patient]. Pick the visits to include." A checklist of the patient's visits not yet invoiced, all ticked; primary "Continue with N visits", plus Cancel. The draft (and its number) is created only on Continue. On desktop the checklist is the top of the invoice editor.
6. **Invoice editor:** number and Draft tag, patient and visit count; line items from the price list (a menu that also offers "+ New price list item"), whole-number quantity, "Add tax (5%)" switch, subtotal and total. Phone: primary "Preview and send", "Save draft" as a text button. Desktop: one page with the form on the left and a **live preview** on the right, primary **Send invoice** at the top right with Download PDF and Mark as sent in a small menu beside it; send blocks show as an inline alert under the preview. Send invoice (and the menu's Mark as sent) asks first in a dialog, "Send INV-0012?", "Download the PDF and send it to Maya Okafor. Once it's marked as sent, it can't be changed.", with Download PDF and primary Mark as sent, because sending can't be undone (owner decision, U11). "Saved on this device" sits beside the title and tag. Locked once sent.
7. **Invoice preview (phone):** the invoice exactly as the patient receives it (style + profile), primary Share PDF, Mark as sent as a text button; send blocks as an inline alert linking to the field to fill in. Desktop has no separate preview screen (see 6). The invoice template lays out a letter page (`8.5in × 11in` aspect ratio with the footer pushed to the bottom). Desktop (`InvoiceFrame`) draws the page at 816px wide and scales it with a CSS transform (`ResizeObserver`) to fit its column, while the phone (`InvoicePreview`, `previewHtml`) displays the 816px page viewport inside an `aspect-[17/22]` full-width `WebView` (scaled down to fit the phone screen width so the preview matches the letter page PDF, rather than showing a reflowed narrow page).
8. **Invoice detail (sent, paid and cancelled):** status tag, the total in large type, number · patient · sent date (paid or cancelled date once paid or cancelled), visits (a cancelled invoice shows the dates it kept), tax, and primary **Record payment** while waiting, with a text button **Cancel invoice** beside or below it. Cancel opens a confirm ("Cancel INV-0012?", "Its visits go back to Ready to invoice. INV-0012 stays in your records as cancelled and won't be used again.", **Keep invoice** / **Cancel invoice** in the danger style) and a toast "Invoice cancelled". A cancelled invoice is read-only, and its PDF carries a "Cancelled" mark; "Marked paid" has Undo in its toast. Phone: **View PDF** reopens the preview so it can be shared again. Desktop: the invoice itself sits beside the details, with **Download PDF** under it (owner decision, U10).
9. **Invoices:** four pills that pick a group, Not sent yet · Waiting · Paid · Cancelled, each with its count ("Waiting · 2"; screen readers hear "Waiting on payment, 2 invoices"). One group shows at a time, newest first; the list opens on the group asked for (Home's Waiting on payment card) or else the first with invoices (owner decision, U10). Each row has number, patient, total and status tag.
10. **Settings:** rows that show their current value and any problem: Your profile ("Dana Reid · 1 field missing"), Price list and tax ("3 items · Tax 5%"), Invoice style (desktop: "Sky accent · Clean font · logo left"; phone: "Set it on the desktop app"), then **Appearance** (Match system, Light, Dark; default Match system; stored on each device, not synced). Below them, "Signed in as name@example.com" and a quiet "Sign out" button. If some changes haven't reached the server yet, Sign out first asks: "Some changes haven't been saved yet" (they're only on this device and would be lost), with "Stay signed in" (default) and "Sign out anyway". At the bottom middle (desktop): a small wordmark (20px) with Terms and Privacy links that open the website in the browser.
11. **Profile (both apps):** "Fields shown on invoices are required." Full name, Title or certification, Clinic name, Address, Phone, each with a "Show on invoice" switch beside its label. An empty field that's shown on invoices says so under it ("Shown on invoices. Add a phone number.").
12. **Price list and tax:** rows of item name and price ("Changing a price doesn't change invoices you've already made."), default tax rate ("Used when you turn tax on for an invoice. Tax is off by default."), then **Invoice numbers**: the four parts in order as rows (Prefix, Year, Month, Number), each with its example ("INV", "2026", "11", "0042"), a switch for all but Number ("Always shown") and Move earlier / Move later buttons; Prefix text (optional); "Between parts" (-, /, ., None) and "Number length" (7, 07, 007… up to six digits) as pills; "Next invoice number" ("To carry on from invoices you made before Kineō, enter the next number you'd use.", inline error "Start at 42 or higher. Lower numbers are already used."); and on tint "Your next invoice will be INV-2026-0042. Invoices already sent keep their number." One "Save settings" for tax and numbers. "Add item" opens a sheet on phone (dialog on desktop) with Name, Price and "Save item"; toast "Item added". The same sheet opens from the invoice's "+ New price list item".
13. **Invoice style (desktop only):** logo (Replace, Remove), header layout as picture buttons (logo left or centred), accent colour (Sky, Forest, Ocean, Plum, Charcoal), font (Clean, Serif, Nunito), footer note, optional footer logo (centred), and a "Show 'Made with Kineō'" switch (on by default), with a live preview beside them; the note "Applies to every invoice in Kineō, including ones already sent. PDFs you've already shared don't change." (a style isn't stored per invoice, so a change restyles sent invoices in the app too); Save style at the top right.
14. **States:**
    - Loading skeletons shaped like the screen they stand in for (real heading, tab bar and back arrow stay live), shown only after 300ms and kept at least 400ms; static under reduced motion.
    - Pending actions show in the button, which keeps its width and ignores repeat taps: "Saving…", "Sending…", "Preparing PDF…".
    - First sign-in on a device: "Getting your patients and invoices" and "This only happens once on this phone (computer). Keep Kineō open until it finishes."
    - A new invoice not on the server yet: "Offline · saved on this device", number "Given when you're online", and "Waiting to reach the server before you can send. Your invoice is safe on this device." above the disabled send button.
    - Desktop sign-in check: the wordmark and a spinner; "Signing you in" only after 2 seconds.
    - "You're all caught up" empty states and inline errors that say what to do.
    - Toasts (`role="status"`): "Note saved", "Invoice sent", "Marked paid" with Undo, "Patient added", "Profile saved", "Item added". A toast with Undo stays until it's dismissed or used.
15. **Sign in:** shown before anything else until signed in. "Kineō" small above the heading "Sign in"; email code first ("Email me a code", then "Check your email" with the code field and "Sign in"), with "Use a password instead" as the alternative. Errors appear under the field they belong to and say what to do; if the sign-in service can't be reached, a short "Can't reach sign-in" screen with "Try again". Under "Email me a code", **Continue with Google** (desktop: "Finish signing in with Google in your browser. Kineō opens again when you're done."). Below, "New to Kineō? **Create an account**": "Create your account" with Full name, Email and "I agree to the Terms and Privacy policy" (a required checkbox, with "Read the Terms and Privacy policy" links on the line below), **Email me a code**, Continue with Google and "I already have an account"; then "Check your email" with the code and **Create account**. The full name becomes Your profile's full name. A new Google account that hasn't agreed yet gets **One more step** (the same checkbox, Create account, "Back to sign in").

Removed in v2: quick chips on the note box, Copy last note, Clear, and the desktop's separate invoice preview screen.

## Business rules

- Notes and invoices are separate; a note can be invoiced later. One invoice can cover several visits.
- The patient handles payment and insurance claims. "Paid" is only for the practitioner's tax records.
- Tax is off by default and optional per invoice.
- Invoices, PDFs and anything sent to a patient show visit dates and services only, never note text.

## Visual style

- HeroUI conventions: 12–16px radius, soft shadows, grey-filled inputs that turn to the surface colour with a primary border on focus, pill chips and status tags, sentence case, no uppercase, Inter.
- **Light and dark.** Both palettes live in `@kineo/tokens` (`colors`, `darkColors`); the Appearance setting picks one, following the system by default.
  - Light: brand sky `#0284C7` for focus, active tab, icons and the macron (not text); buttons and links `#0369A1`, hover `#075985`, white button text; text on tint `#075985`, tint `#E0F2FE`, page `#F7FBFF`, surface `#FFFFFF`, input fill `#F4F4F5`, text `#11181C`, muted `#71717A`, placeholders `#67676F`, borders `#E4E4E7`, error `#B42318`.
  - Dark: page `#0B1117` (not pure black), surface `#151C24` with a 1px ring `#232D38`, input fill `#232D38`, divider `#2B3540`, text `#E6EDF3`, muted `#9AA4AF`, placeholders `#8B95A0`; accent sky `#38BDF8` for buttons (dark text `#082F49`), focus and icons, links `#7DD3FC`. Depth comes from lighter surfaces and the ring, not shadows. Toasts are light on dark.
  - Invoice previews, invoices and accent swatches stay on white paper with their own colours in both modes.
- **Status tags** (`statusTags`, `darkStatusTags`): Not invoiced (grey, dashed circle), Draft (sky, pencil), Waiting on payment (amber, clock), Paid (green, check), Cancelled (grey, crossed-out circle), Error (red, alert). Every tag has an icon and a word, so status never depends on colour alone.
- **Input edge.** Inputs have a visible border, `inputEdge` (`#878791` light, `#6B7684` dark; at least 3:1 on the surfaces inputs sit on). It is also the switch's off track and the funding chip's outline. On focus the field turns to the surface colour with a sky border.
- **Funding chip** (`fundingChipIcons`, `chipText`): an outlined grey pill with an icon and a word, never tinted, so it can't be read as the sky Draft tag. It says who is billed (the funder's name, or Self-pay); an ended funding has a dashed outline and "Ended 31 Mar 2027". Chips say who is billed; status tags say what state something is in. Neither wraps.
- **Spacing:** 8px inside a group, 16px between rows, 24–32px between groups.
- One filled button per screen; secondary actions (Save draft, Cancel, Replace note) are text buttons. Focus ring 2px sky, at least 3:1 against what it touches. Every text colour reaches 4.5:1 on its background.
- Rejected directions: top app bar, desktop sidebar, MUI (too blocky), terracotta palette.
