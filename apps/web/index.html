What it does

Visit notes

Find your patient and write today's note, by typing or dictating. Copy your last note to start from, or add to or replace an earlier one. Every visit keeps its own note and edit time.

Invoicing

Put one or more visits on an invoice, pick line items from your own price list and add tax if you need it. Preview it exactly as your patient will see it, send it, and record the payment when it comes in.

Works offline

Kineō keeps your notes and invoices on your computer and phone, so it works without a connection and syncs between your devices when you're back online.

Built for solo physios in BC

Kineō is made for physiotherapists in British Columbia who run their own practice. It does two things, notes and invoices, and keeps everything else out of the way.

Home shows who you're seeing and what needs doing: visits ready to invoice, invoices not sent yet and invoices waiting on payment. Your patients handle their own payment and insurance claims; Kineō keeps a record of what's been paid for your taxes.

It runs on your Mac or Windows computer and on your iPhone or Android phone.

Your patients' privacy

  • Your notes and invoices are stored on your own devices first, and sync over an encrypted connection.
  • Every record is locked to your account. No one else using Kineō can see or change your patients' information.
  • Sign-in is handled by Clerk, which holds your login details only. Patient information never goes to Clerk.
  • Kineō never submits insurance claims or sends patient records anywhere on your behalf.
